    Milestones

    From Wenzhou to the World — 38 Years of Excellence

    1987
    1987
    Founded in Wenzhou
    Established the predecessor — Xiaojiang wire-drawing factory in Wenzhou, officially beginning the entrepreneurial journey.
    1991
    First Expansion
    Secured 3-mu industrial land, renamed Pingyang No.10 Plastics Factory, specializing in woven plastic packaging.
    1993
    Northeast Market Entry
    Expanded into Northeast China, becoming the designated packaging supplier for multiple enterprises across three provinces.
    1997
    Southern Marketing Hub
    Established the Southern regional marketing center in Cangnan, completing the domestic market layout.
    2001
    Scale-up & Incorporation
    Acquired 50-mu land in Xiaojing, founded Zhejiang Litian Plastics Co., Ltd., achieving scaled production.
    2004
    Diversification
    Expanded into cotton spinning, packaging printing, and import-export trade, filling a local industry gap in Pingyang.
    2006
    Core Patent
    Developed long-short fiber blended knot-free pure cotton yarn process, earning a core invention patent.
    2009
    Group Formation
    Officially upgraded to DongDa Co., Ltd., building a diversified industrial structure; launched eco-home textile business.
    2010
    Xinjiang Aid Mission
    Responded to the national Xinjiang aid initiative, entered Aksu Textile Industrial City as a key Zhejiang enterprise, founding Xinjiang Litian Textile Co.
    2012
    Raw Material Base
    Built a 30,000-mu cotton planting base and two automated ginning mills, securing the upstream supply chain.
    2015
    Cotton Museum
    China's first enterprise-level cotton quilt home textile museum opened, preserving industrial heritage.
    2017
    Northwest Scale Leader
    Founded Jiangsu New Materials Co., introduced full smart production lines, becoming the largest woven packaging enterprise in Northwest China.
    2019
    Packaging Culture Museum
    China's first enterprise packaging culture museum completed, celebrating and passing on the industry's cultural legacy.
    2020
    Circular Economy
    Xintian Technology launched, focusing on recycling and circular economy for a green cotton-textile supply chain.
    2021
    Smart Manufacturing
    China's first smart woven packaging production line commissioned, entering the intelligent manufacturing era.
    2022
    New Material R&D
    Eco-friendly anti-oxidation packaging material developed and launched to market.
    2023
    R&D Task Force
    Established industry-university-research team, tackling core technologies for eco-friendly high-strength packaging.
    2024
    Central Asia Entry
    Seized Belt & Road opportunities, established Boxin Co. in Uzbekistan, penetrating Central Asian markets for cement, food & full-category packaging.
    2025
    Kazakhstan Expansion
    Established Dongda Co. in Kazakhstan, focusing on high-end custom packaging for cement and chemicals, partnering with local industry leaders.
    2026
    Deepening Global Strategy
    Scaling Central Asian operations, deepening core client matrix, standardizing team management, and continuously growing market share across the region.
